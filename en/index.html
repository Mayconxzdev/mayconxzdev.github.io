HOW I WORK

From a loosely defined request to a supported system.

I talk with the people who use the process, map exceptions and turn the need into a solution that can be deployed, explained and monitored.
  1. 01

    Context

    I understand the need, users, environment and expected outcome.

  2. 02

    Process and risks

    I map the current flow, exceptions, sensitive data, dependencies and failure points.

  3. 03

    Architecture and rules

    I define the source of truth, permissions, approvals, integrations and recovery.

  4. 04

    Build

    I develop the system, automation, interface, persistence and contracts.

  5. 05

    Deployment

    I install, configure, document and train the people who will use it.

  6. 06

    Evolution
