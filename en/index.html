WHEN SYSTEMS MEET REALITY

Failures, diagnosis and correction are part of the technical evidence.

01Mala Direta

Campaign cancellation during queue processing

Situation
A campaign continued until the first batch of 15 messages completed after cancellation was requested.
Diagnosis and correction
I stopped the workflow, diagnosed the already-processing state and changed the architecture to revalidate status and eligibility before each recipient.
Lesson
Cancellation must be a rule checked inside the loop, not only at campaign start.
02Postagem Redes

Meta media-download timeout

Situation
After a successful validation, a later six-slide attempt exceeded the public tunnel download time.
Diagnosis and correction
I isolated the external cause and documented the evolution: stable HTTPS origin, optimized media and per-network validation.
Lesson
A validated integration still needs appropriate infrastructure for continuous operation.
