Context and problem

Searching templates, copying email data, reusing documents and preparing messages created manual steps and risked using another client’s information.

02

My responsibility

Requirements gathering, full development, deployment on 4 computers, documentation, training, user review and support.

03

Architecture and decisions

Python, PySide6, SQLite, IMAP/SMTP, LibreOffice for ODT/PDF, network storage and PyInstaller.

04

Results and evidence

  • Used daily by 4 professionals.
  • Supports 3 to 5 proposals per day.
  • Simple proposals reduced from 2-4 minutes to under 30 seconds.

Declared limitations

How I would evolve it at larger scale

Public evidence contains no credentials, personal data or sensitive internal information.
