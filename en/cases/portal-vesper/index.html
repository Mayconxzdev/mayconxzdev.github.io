VISUAL CONTEXT

From an internal system to a multi-company platform

The screens below belong to the previous public reference. They show domains and journeys being reassessed, not the final visual state of the new product.

What is already demonstrated in the new foundation

Tenancy and RLS in the first capability pack, versioned Action Envelopes, immutable approvals, idempotency, timeline, auditing, outbox, RFQs, offers and supplier comparison in sandbox.

What is not yet demonstrated

Enterprise pilot, multi-tenant production, real external side effects, scale, availability and commercial operation.

01

Product thesis

Portal does not try to replace ERP, CRM, service desk and communication platforms at once. It starts as an operational layer across existing systems and assumes capabilities only after demonstrating maturity.

02

What I am building

A multi-tenant kernel with organizations, people, roles, objects, relationships, timeline, auditing, files, Action Envelopes, approvals, observable integrations and capability packs.

03

How it is architected

A modular monolith using React/TypeScript and FastAPI/PostgreSQL, Alembic migrations, tenant/RLS isolation, transactional outbox, workers and n8n as an external API-based executor, never the source of truth.

04

Real state

  • Procurement Intake and sourcing implemented and validated in sandbox.
  • Approvals linked to version and hash, with material changes invalidating prior decisions.
  • Being prepared for an internal pilot; not presented as multi-tenant production.

Disclosure boundary

The current repository is private during the refactor. The public link shows the previous architecture and must not be confused with the current canonical foundation.

Next gate

Complete foundation validation, define the pilot process and users, integrate a real System of Record with reconciliation and measure adoption, cycle time, errors and support.

Status is declared by level: specified, implemented, tested, executed and user-validated are not treated as synonyms.
