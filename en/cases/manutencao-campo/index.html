01

The problem

Maintenance on machines and equipment needs to be recorded so the team can review completed work and follow its history.

02

What I did

I built and use an application at work to record maintenance on equipment. I also prepared an independent public demonstration, with no company data or connection to its environment.

03

How I built it

React/TypeScript, FastAPI, asynchronous SQLAlchemy, SQLite, PWA, hashes and dossier generation.

04

What it solves

  • Keeps maintenance records organized for the team to consult.
  • The public demo makes the workflows understandable without exposing company information.

Limits of this version
