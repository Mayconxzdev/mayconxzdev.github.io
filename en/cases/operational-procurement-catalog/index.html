Public limit

The internal base originated from a spreadsheet maintained for approximately two years, but volumes, values and suppliers are not published.

01

Context and problem

The information existed, but finding an item required knowing its spreadsheet tab, family or exact description. Concurrent edits could also overwrite newer work.

02

What I did

Modeled the catalog and implemented the API, search, entry, history, backups, revision control, optional OCR, sanitized public edition and local deployment.

03

How I built it

FastAPI serves the interface and contracts; versioned JSON remains the readable source and SQLite FTS5 is a rebuildable derived index. Each write validates the loaded revision.

04

What is demonstrated

  • Daily use by three operational users and management consultation.
  • Fast search by code, name or supplier.
  • Edit conflicts, backups and reproducible tests in the public release.

What this case does not claim

It is not an ERP, fiscal module, payment platform or large-scale enterprise system.

Responsible evolution

Higher scale would require a central transactional database, corporate identity, role-based authorization, HTTPS, observability and a monitored backup and restore policy.

The publication contains no company materials, suppliers, prices, paths, credentials or history.
