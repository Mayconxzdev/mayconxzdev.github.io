Purchasing and Quotes

From request to supplier follow-up

I built a desktop application to organize purchase requests, supplier quotes and replies. Instead of tracking every step in separate spreadsheets and emails, the team can work through one flow from the request to the response.

Where it is usedPurchasing operations
What it organizesSuppliers · requests · quotes · replies
Key safeguardReview before sending

IN DAILY USE

Prepare and follow up

The team organizes request details and tracks replies without searching through separate email threads.
01

The problem

Supplier details, requests, attachments and replies were spread across spreadsheets, emails and messages. It was hard to find a contact and tell who had responded.

02

What I built

I created a Windows application to search suppliers, prepare quote requests and purchase orders, review outgoing messages and track replies.

03

How it works

A person chooses the request type, enters the necessary details, checks recipients and attachments, and then sends it. The application organizes replies and shows what still needs attention.

04

Who uses it

The tool supports the purchasing team when supplier quotes are needed.

05

The benefit

Requests, messages and replies stay connected. The team spends less time switching between files and emails and can pick up follow-up work more easily.

06

The challenge

The app needs to handle replies that arrive later and temporary email failures. It keeps local history and a queue so work can resume safely.

Technology

Python · PySide6 · SQLite · IMAP/SMTP · Excel · Windows.

Public demo

The screens use fictional data. Sending and network synchronization are disabled in this version; no supplier or company document is published.

I built the tool around a real purchasing workflow and continue to evolve it with the people who use it.
